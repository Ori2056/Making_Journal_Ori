### Defining scope and recreating project statement

Using the template provided on the class slides I input the work from previous weeks as well as my reflective proposal into NotebookLM and it resulted with this:

In this project, I have developed an interactive data visualization designed as a constantly evolving web. This work serves as a direct visualization of social networks, using live data streams harvested from social media, text forums, and other online text-based content. My focus is on interpersonal connection, specifically gauging how and when people come together to bond over shared interests or experiences. By transforming these thoughts and sensations into non-static, visual outputs, I aim to recontextualize our digital footprints into a more fluid representation of human interaction.
This work is grounded in the principles of data humanism, which prioritizes the deep relationships between data points and the human experience. Through a process I call "vibe coding," I have moved beyond the limitations of traditional programming to create a variety of interactive shapes with varied movements and speeds. This approach allows me to engage critically with ideas of interactivity and multi-modality, particularly regarding accessibility factors such as proximity and disability. I believe the cyclical nature of this design process is heavily introspective, allowing for a reflexive look at our daily subconscious practices.
My work responds to a contemporary scenario where younger generations are becoming increasingly socially isolated due to the manufactured comfort of online engagement. By projecting these social insights, I hope to bridge the gaps in social technology that make contact over devices feel limited. Ultimately, this visualization is intended to crumble the appeal of the digital bubble, incentivizing the public to build deeper, interpersonal relationships outside of the internet. I want viewers to reflect on their own experiences and feel empowered to navigate social circumstances more authentically, both online and in real life.
